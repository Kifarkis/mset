Electronic signatures for regulated work. Signers sign by email link, PIN or electronic ID, and every completed document is sealed, timestamped and carries its audit trail.

Meeting transcription for teams that cannot send recordings just anywhere - Teams meetings, and meetings in person or on the phone recorded with Plaud. Summaries and actions, shared with the people you choose.

  • Your meetings stay in your own Microsoft 365
  • Not used for training
